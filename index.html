Thesis: prioritise, package, scale

Governments drive a large share of payment flows, and enabler partners already sit inside the agencies that move this money.

1

Prioritise

Build one ranked pipeline of partners, use cases and markets with the in-market VGS sales leads, so that each partner has a clear reason to work with Visa and a named first opportunity.

2

Package

Turn each priority use case into a joint solution that combines Visa capabilities with the partner platform, and feed the gaps back to the Visa solutions teams for the product roadmap.

3

Scale

Own the partners that already operate in several Asia Pacific markets with account plans, contracting, onboarding and joint RFP strategies, so that one agreement opens several markets.

Enabler partner lifecycle

One motion for every partner, so that the in-market sales leads know what to expect at each step.

1. IdentifyMap partners by use case, market and agency footprint with the in-market VGS leads.Measure: ranked partner list
2. QualifyCheck government references, multi-market reach, bank relationships and integration readiness.Measure: scorecard tier
3. StructureAgree the commercial or strategic agreement, the joint value proposition and the first target agencies.Measure: signed agreement
4. OnboardComplete contracting, supplier onboarding, certification and sales enablement for both teams.Measure: time to first joint pitch
5. Co-sellRun joint account plans and RFP strategies, with clear roles for Visa, the partner and the bank.Measure: joint pipeline and RFPs
6. ScaleRepeat the solution in the next market and feed product gaps to the Visa solutions teams.Measure: markets live, volume

RFP and deal structuring levers

These are the ways VGS can support partner-led bids and the commercial frameworks to choose from. Each lever needs approval from the relevant Visa teams.

Partner-led RFP support

  • Bid or no-bid review with the partner and the in-market sales lead
  • Clear roles for Visa, the partner and the issuing or acquiring bank in the response
  • Visa solution content, security and compliance answers, and case studies
  • Joint pricing logic and a value case for the agency, such as cost to collect or time to disburse
  • Early engagement with the agency through the partner, inside procurement rules
  • Post-award plan for launch, onboarding and adoption

Commercial and contracting frameworks

  • Referral or reseller agreement with a revenue share on volume
  • Co-investment in go-to-market, such as joint events, pilots and marketing funds
  • Technology partnership with API integration and service level commitments
  • Programme enrolment, such as Visa Ready, with certification steps
  • Strategic agreement for multi-market partners with an annual joint business plan
  • Review and exit clauses tied to the partner scorecard

First 90 days, proposed

A plan to test with the team, built around the role scope.

Days 1 to 30: listen and map

Meet the in-market VGS sales leads, the solutions teams and global VGS colleagues. Review the signed partners and the live RFPs. Build the first ranked list of partners, use cases and markets.

Days 31 to 60: prioritise and package

Agree the top partners per market with the sales leads. Write the joint value propositions for two or three priority use cases, and open structured discussions with the first multi-market partners.

Days 61 to 90: prove it in the field

Start joint account plans with the priority partners, support the first joint RFP response, and set a quarterly review cadence with partner scorecards for leadership.
